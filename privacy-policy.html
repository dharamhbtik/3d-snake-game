Privacy Policy for Snake 3D

Realistic Farmland & Crop Field Edition

Effective Date: August 25, 2026 Last Updated: August 25, 2026 Application: Snake 3D
🛡️ Summary: Snake 3D is a standalone offline game. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, telemetry, location data, or device identifiers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Snake 3D ("the Application", "we", "our") handles user information when you download, install, and play the game distributed via the Microsoft Store.

We are dedicated to respecting and protecting your privacy. This game has been developed with a privacy-by-design architecture, ensuring complete offline functionality and zero data harvesting.

2. Information Collection and Use

A. Personal Information

Snake 3D does NOT collect any personally identifiable information (PII), such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, photos, or payment details.

B. Gameplay & Diagnostic Data

The game operates entirely locally on your device. We do not transmit gameplay metrics, session durations, or crash logs to any external remote servers.

C. Third-Party Analytics and Advertising

Snake 3D contains NO third-party advertising SDKs, no tracking pixels, and no commercial analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Facebook SDK). Your gameplay activity is strictly private.

3. Local Device Storage

The Application uses standard local application storage on your device solely for essential gameplay functionality:

  • Local High Scores: Saves your highest score locally on your device.
  • User Preferences: Remembers your chosen game speed (Relaxed, Normal, Fast) and sound mute/unmute preferences.

This data remains strictly on your local device and is never uploaded to any cloud service or third-party servers.

4. Device Permissions

Snake 3D requires no sensitive device permissions. It does not request or require access to:

  • Location Services (GPS or Network)
  • Camera or Microphone
  • Contacts or Address Book
  • Photos, Media Library, or External Storage
  • Network communication or background internet services

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Snake 3D is rated for all ages (General Audience / Everyone). We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and global children's safety regulations. Because our application does not collect any personal data from any user, we do not knowingly or unknowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.

6. Security of Your Information

Because no personal or sensitive data is collected or transmitted across the internet, your information is naturally protected against unauthorized access, data interception, and server breaches.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect future game enhancements or regulatory requirements. Any revisions will be updated on this page with a refreshed "Last Updated" timestamp. We encourage users to review this page periodically.
