# Privacy Policy for Snake 3D (Microsoft Store)

**Effective Date:** August 25, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 25, 2026  
**Publisher:** Snake 3D Development Team  
**Distribution Channel:** Microsoft Store / Windows App Store  

---

## 1. Overview
This Privacy Policy outlines how **Snake 3D** ("the Application", "we", "our") handles user information when downloaded and played from the Microsoft Store.

We operate under a strict **Privacy by Design** standard. Snake 3D is a standalone offline game that does **NOT collect, transmit, store, sell, or share** any personally identifiable information (PII), tracking data, or device analytics.

---

## 2. Information Collection & Usage

### A. Personal Data
Snake 3D does **not** request, access, or collect any personal details such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, contact lists, photos, or payment information.

### B. Telemetry & Analytics
The application contains **no tracking pixels, telemetry probes, or third-party analytics services** (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase, or Facebook SDK). All gameplay metrics remain strictly local.

### C. Advertising
Snake 3D is completely **ad-free**. There are no third-party ad networks or tracking libraries included.

---

## 3. Local Storage Disclosures
The game uses local device storage solely for core game settings:
- **Local High Scores:** Saves your highest score locally on your device.
- **Player Preferences:** Remembers your selected speed setting (Relaxed, Normal, Fast) and sound mute toggle.

This data never leaves your physical device and is removed if you uninstall the application.

---

## 4. Permissions & Device Access
Snake 3D requests **zero sensitive permissions**. It does not access:
- ❌ Location services (GPS / Network)
- ❌ Camera or Microphone
- ❌ Contacts or Calendar
- ❌ Photos, Documents, or Media Libraries
- ❌ Background network communication

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
Snake 3D is rated for all audiences (ESRB Everyone / PEGI 3). We fully comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and global child safety standards. Because we collect zero data from all players, we do not collect personal information from children under 13.
